# Phase 20 — Demo Curriculum Report

## Content policy
All Phase 20 curriculum examples are original, brief staging content authored for the project. They are explicitly labeled `DEMO CURRICULUM` / `DEMO BOOK`; they are not commercial textbook excerpts and do not claim to represent a selected publisher or proprietary source.

## Coverage
- Existing Pre-A1 starter course plus short original A1, A2 and B1 communication courses cover introductions, polite repetition, routine work updates, plan changes and neutral safety reporting.
- Sample lessons include objectives, examples, vocabulary translations, content blocks and required multiple-choice practice.
- One non-commercial demo Book structure maps to existing LMS records.
- QA accounts are tagged by `@demo.example` and the fixture ledger uses `PHASE20_QA_FIXTURE` / `level-demo-seed` markers. Their progress and XP are test values, not actual learner outcomes or analytics.
